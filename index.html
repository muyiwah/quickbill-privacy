QuickBill Privacy Policy

App: QuickBill: Invoice & Receipt (com.finessedev.quickbill) · Developer: GeeksCraft · Effective: 17 November 2026

QuickBill helps small businesses make invoices, receipts and quotes as PDFs and keep track of who owes them money. It is built to keep your business records on your phone. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we (the developer) do not run a server that receives your invoices, customers or payments.

1. Your records stay on your device

  • Your business profile and logo, customers (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses), price-list items, invoices, receipts, quotes, payments and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • PDFs are created on your device.
  • We never see, collect or sell your records, your customers' details or your payments.
  • You decide what is shared. If you add customers to QuickBill, you are responsible for handling their information as the law in your country requires.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data unless you have saved a backup yourself.

2. Data that leaves your device

Only in these cases, and never your records unless you choose to send them:

  • Sharing, WhatsApp and backup: when you share a PDF, send a payment reminder or save a backup file, it goes to the app or location you pick (for example WhatsApp, email, Google Drive, iCloud or Files). For a WhatsApp reminder the customer's phone number is used to open the chat. That service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the free version shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional videos you can choose to watch to unlock the Pro features for 24 hours. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID or the iOS advertising identifier), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in Settings. On iPhone, the app asks permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Purchases (Google Play and Apple App Store): the one-time "lifetime" purchase (no ads, Pro invoice styles, PDFs without the QuickBill line and unlimited customers) is processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive only confirmation that the purchase is valid, not your payment details.

3. Permissions

  • Photos and files: only the logo photo you choose, or the backup file you restore, through the system picker.
  • Biometrics / screen lock (Face ID on iPhone): for the optional App Lock. Your phone checks your fingerprint, face or PIN; the app never receives it.
  • Internet: only for ads and purchases as described above. The app works fully offline without them.

4. Children

QuickBill is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add App Lock to open QuickBill only with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Backup files are not encrypted: keep them somewhere private.

6. Your choices

  • Change ad consent in the app's Settings (where it applies), or remove ads with the lifetime purchase.
  • Delete any invoice, customer or item in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data on the device.
  • Because we hold no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Contact us with any question.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
